Card condition guide
ConditionWhat it looks like
Near MintNM
Looks fresh from the pack. A faint touch on a corner or edge at most.
Lightly PlayedLP
Light wear you notice on a close look. Small edge whitening or a couple of tiny scuffs.
Moderately PlayedMP
Clear wear, still flat and whole. Edge whitening all round, scratches, a soft corner.
Heavily PlayedHP
Heavy wear. Fine in a sleeve. Creases that do not break the surface, rubbing, worn corners.
DamagedDMG
Something is wrong with the card. Tears, water damage, thin spots, ink or marker.
