Privacy

We keep what you type into a form so the shop can answer it. No analytics, no ad trackers.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Kept

What each form stores

Each form saves a row in the shop's database, and nothing else about you.

  • Seat for a game night: the event, your first name, your email, an optional note, and whether you are on the waitlist.
  • Want list: your first name, your email, the game, and the cards you listed.
  • Buylist: your name, your email, cash or store credit, an optional note, and each card row (name, set, condition, quantity).
  • Contact: your name, your email and your message.

Each row also gets the time it was sent. Rows stay until the store deletes them.

Never

What we do not do

  • No analytics and no advertising trackers.
  • No accounts and no cookies set by this site.
  • The one thing saved in your browser is your Motion on or off choice, and it never leaves your device.
  • Fonts and images are served from this site, not from another company.

Instagram posts on the home page load from Instagram only after you tap to show one.

Links to Google Maps, Instagram, X, Patreon, TopDeck and Wizards open other sites with their own rules.

Yours

Ask us to delete your data

Use the contact form: give the email you used and what to remove. We will delete those rows.
