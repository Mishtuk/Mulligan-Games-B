# Spec: Top release panel, product page, less-bland pass (designer -> mediator -> coder)
Owner 2026-11-20: "a little panel at the top of the screen... for the new releases, like a little switch tab"; site "looks very bland and plain"; wants "more images". Rules 2, 3, 12 (release exception), 13 apply. Dark default; every token below is an existing one, light via `[data-theme=light]`. No new facts: every word comes from `releases.json` and `product-contents.json`.

## 1. Top release panel (`.top-rel`, above the Home hero, below the header)
Data: `shownReleases()` (same order as the lower section: Star Trek, then date). 5 tabs today.
- **Tabs row**: `role=tablist` `aria-label="New releases"`, one `role=tab` per release, label = `short` or game name (Magic, Pokemon, Riftbound, Hololive, One Piece), mono 13px, 48px tall, horizontal scroll with `scroll-snap` at 390 (no wrap, no scrollbar shown, right fade hint). Selected tab: `--accent` fill, `--on-accent` ink, 3px bottom border removed so it joins the panel; others `--card`. `aria-selected`, roving `tabindex`; Left/Right/Home/End move AND select (automatic activation, panels are static, so no cost). Focus ring 3px `--link`.
- **No auto-rotate, no timer, no Play/Pause.** The lower New releases ad keeps the cycling (spec-rotation unchanged). Choice is per visit only.
- **Panel** (`role=tabpanel`, `aria-labelledby` its tab, `hidden` on the rest), the release's own `data-theme` as in `.rel-feat`:
  - Key art `image` fills the panel as a 16:7 crop (`object-fit:cover`, `object-position:center`), real `<picture>` webp sources, `width/height` attributes set, `alt=""` (name is in the text beside it), first panel `fetchpriority=high`, others `loading=lazy`.
  - Over the lower third of the art: a solid `--card` caption strip (never text on art): game badge, set name (h2, 20px), status line ("Out now" / "Releases Jan 29" from `releaseDate` vs today; Pokemon and Star Trek read "Out now"), and the link "See what's in the box" (button style, 48px) to `/shop/<id>/`.
  - Credit line 11px mono under the art, using `credit` (2 lines max at 390).
- **Height budget**: 390 wide: tabs 48 + art 169 (16:7 of 358) + caption 96 + credit 20 = ~333px, plus the 56px header = 389 of an ~700px viewport. The hero ("Pull up a chair") and the first binder pocket therefore **move below the fold at 390**; the hero H1 starts at ~400px and is still partly visible, the first pocket does not. Acceptable because the owner asked for more images up top and this is the top-priority ad; to limit loss, trim hero top padding to 16px at 390 and keep the pocket tap target in the 2nd screen. If the coder cannot keep the budget, shorten art to 21:9 before dropping the panel. At 768: art left 50%, caption right (row layout), ~220px tall; 1280: same, 240px, centered in the 1120 container, pocket visible.
- **No-JS**: the script adds `.js` to `<html>`; without it all five panels are NOT hidden by CSS... only the first is shown, and the tab row renders as a plain `<ul>` of links to `/shop/<id>/` (2 columns at 390, 48px rows). Tab row is built from the same markup (`<a href="/shop/<id>/">` upgraded to `role=tab` by JS, `href` kept for middle-click).
- **Dark and light**: panel border 3px `--line`, hard shadow `--accent2`; art has no overlay; caption `--card`/`--text`. Contrast checked by tokens already in spec-dark. Reduced motion: no transition (instant swap); otherwise 150ms opacity.
- **Budget**: ~1.1k gzip of CSS+HTML plus 0.5k JS; reuse `.rel-feat` classes and the existing art. D12 cap is 23,200 gzip: needs a new D-entry from the boss before build. Offset: lower New releases grid drops the duplicate full-width art (see 3).

## 2. Product page `/shop/<release-id>/` (static, one per release, 5 pages, in sitemap)
`<title>` "Set name: what's in the box | Mulligan Games"; one `<h1>` = set name; own `data-theme`.
Order top to bottom:
1. **Breadcrumb** `Home / Shop / Set name` (`nav aria-label=Breadcrumb`, last `aria-current=page`), 44px targets; "Back to Shop" is the middle crumb, no second button.
2. **Hero**: key art (credited) full width; below it at 390 the h1, game badge, status chip (Out now / date, plus prerelease date when set) and facts line (`types`). At 768+: art left 60%, product shot (`product` image, credited) in a drawn binder-pocket frame right 40%, h1 and CTA beside.
3. **Hold CTA** "Hold one for me" (existing `cta` text) opens/links the existing hold form prefilled with the release id; under it a one-line "Holds are not sales. Pay at the counter." (existing policy copy only).
4. **What's in the box**: one card per product in `product-contents.json`: name (h3), type chip, contents as a short list with counts (`30 Play Boosters`, `14 cards per pack`), `msrpUsd` as sticker price chip only if not null, `certainty` as a plain word. Grid: 1 col at 390, 2 at 768, 3 at 1280; card = One Pocket frame without the 5:7 ratio. Cap 12 cards (Star Trek has 12); source link in the footer line of the section, not per card.
5. **Set summary**: `ad` line + `types` as 2 to 3 sentences from existing data; nothing invented.
6. **Related events**: events in `events.json` whose `title`/`game` matches the release (prerelease, release night), as the existing ticket card, link to Events; none: the section is omitted, not shown empty.
7. **Other releases**: the other 4 as thumbnail cards (art 320w, name, status, link), 2 cols at 390, 4 at 1280.
8. **Credit and fine print**: `credit`, `sourceUrl` (opens new tab, labelled), "not affiliated" line (footer already carries it).
- **Sticky bar (phones, under 768px only)**: bottom 0, 64px, `--card` with 3px top border, "Hold one for me" button 48px full width minus 16px gutters, `padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`; sits left of the sun/moon button (button moves up 72px on these pages); hidden while the hold form is on screen (IntersectionObserver; without JS it stays). `body` bottom padding 72px so the credit is never covered.
- **Empty states**: product with `contents` ["contents not announced"] or null: card shows the name, type chip and "Contents not announced yet. Ask us at the counter." (Riftbound Legacy, Hololive, parts of One Piece). A release with no `product` image: the drawn booster box/pack from the Shop pocket art replaces the product shot (generic shapes, rule 12). No price: no chip.
- **Images (only what we have)**: Star Trek 960 key + 480 bundle; Pokemon 578 key + 360 box; Riftbound 960 roadmap (art only); Hololive 960 art (no product shot); One Piece only a 160px box (never upscaled; show at 160 in the pocket frame, art slot uses the drawn One Piece theme panel). Hero art `sizes="(min-width:768px) 60vw, 100vw"`.
- All New releases ads, the top panel, lower rel-feat CTA "Hold one for me" secondary link, and Shop release pockets link to `/shop/<id>/`; the hold form stays on the Shop/Visit flow as now.

## 3. Less-bland pass (small, specific; use only the files we have)
- **Home**: hero gets a drawn counter strip (binder + toploader + dice row, existing drawn family) behind the heading in `--bg2`; Binder week pockets each get a tiny drawn game glyph (sleeve, deck, dice) in the pocket corner; the Instagram strip keeps the two flyers as 480w thumbs, enlarged to 2 columns at 390; the lower New releases grid uses the `product` shot (not the key art again) on each card.
- **Shop**: release pockets show the real `product` shot instead of a plain box (One Piece 160px at native size); each section heading gets a 4px accent stripe and a drawn shelf line; singles case keeps the drawn case, add a taped price sticker sheet.
- **Events**: the championships and series Instagram flyers (credited, placeholder, confirm-with-store flag, rule 13) as 360px side images on the Championships and Series sections at 768+, stacked and 480w at 390; pairings sheet gets a taped drawn "clipboard" header and alternate ruled rows tinted `--bg2`.
- **Sell**: a drawn buylist receipt beside the form at 768+; sleeve/binder row illustration above the headline (drawn only, no card art).
- **Visit**: the drawn storefront is enlarged to the full width of the page header at 390 with the hours sheet taped beside it at 768+; waiting on owner shop photos, so each photo slot is a labelled drawn frame ready to swap.
- **Product pages** themselves are the biggest image gain (real art plus box shots); panels tinted per game theme; footer gets a thin drawn shelf strip across all pages.
- **Rules**: no publisher logos as branding; no card scans; no customer faces in Instagram placeholders; all added drawn art is inline SVG or CSS, <1.5k gzip total per page; each page checked against D7/D12 and Home needs the D-entry above; images lazy except the first top-panel art; `width`/`height` set to avoid layout shift.
