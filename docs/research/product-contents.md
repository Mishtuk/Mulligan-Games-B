## Magic: Star Trek (mtg-star-trek) - Confirmed
Source: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/collecting-star-trek (cross-checked with .../star-trek-buyers-guide and magic.wizards.com/en/products/star-trek). Set release 2026-11-13; prerelease 11-06 to 11-12. No per-product dates on the page.
- Play Booster Box: 30 Play Boosters. Pack: 14 cards (6-7 C, 3 U, 1 wildcard, 1 R/M, 1 trad. foil, 1 land) + 1 token. Pack MSRP $6.99. Box MSRP not stated.
- Collector Booster Box: 12 packs of 15 cards. Pack MSRP $37.99. Signed Headliners in extremely low quantity.
- Bundle $69.99: 9 Play Boosters, 15 foil + 15 non-foil basics, 1 foil promo, 2 reference cards, oversized spindown life counter, storage box.
- Beam Me Up Bundle $99.99: 8 Play Boosters, 1 Collector Booster, 15 foil + 15 non-foil basics, 2 of 8 foil promos, 2 reference cards, spindown counter, storage box.
- Commander Decks (Federation Fleet, Landing Party, Klingon Fury, We Are the Borg) $74.99: 100 cards, foil borderless commander, 32 new-to-Magic, 10 tokens, reference card, deck box. Collector's Edition (surge foil) $159.99.
- Draft Night $119.99: 12 Play, 1 Collector, 90 basics, 10 tokens, draft insert.
- Scene Boxes (Enterprise-Q, Divergent Timeline) $41.99: 3 Play Boosters, 6 foil borderless scene cards, 6 art cards, easel.
- Welcome Deck (5 mono-color 40-card decks, MSRP not stated); Prerelease Pack (6 Play Boosters, foil rare/mythic, deck box, spindown die; MSRP not stated).
- Discrepancy on the page: Commander new-card count is "32" in one place and "31 rare and 2 mythic" in another; JSON uses 32.
