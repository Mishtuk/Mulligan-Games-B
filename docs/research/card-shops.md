## Patterns worth using
- Search box first, then next events and hours chip (Misty Mountain, Journey's End).
- Events with price, format, pre-register/ticket (Zulu's, Nutmeg, Wizard's Tower); one row per night.
- Sell/buylist page with rates, condition guide, credit vs cash, bulk rates (Nutmeg).
- Say table count and capacity, house rules, hours incl. holidays (Cardsurfer, Labyrinth).
- Discord link in header and footer; real address, phone, map, directions.
- Proof badges as counts only (TCGplayer sales, Google rating); no reviewer names.
- Platform is rarely the differentiator: Shopify + BinderPOS is the most common stack; Lightspeed and CrystalCommerce also seen.

## Avoid
- Single long events page with stale dates; hours or address missing; template-only look with no photos.
- Duplicated nav or banners, typos, outdated news; buylist or credit rules hidden in an FAQ.
- Separate sites for shop and buylist that need two logins (Wizard's Tower) unless clearly explained.
